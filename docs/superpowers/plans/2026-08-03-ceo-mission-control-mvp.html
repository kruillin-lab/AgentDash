Stop conditions

Three gates control the entire build.

A user approval can authorize a known action. It cannot turn an unproven technical capability into a pass.

Unlocks only after exact-once Hermes and CodeG lifecycle proofs plus restart reconciliation.

Requires: atomic keys, runtime, cancel, identity, receipt

Launch policy

Cheap is not the same as safe.

Auto launch

All conditions must be mechanically true at launch time.

  • Maximum cost ≤ $1 with a hard route-specific bound
  • Maximum runtime ≤ 30 minutes and enforced inside CodeG
  • Cancellation and bounded stop latency proven
  • Read-only or validated isolated worktree
  • No production, publish, payment, messaging, credential, or destructive capability
  • Card, TaskSpec, policy, and launch intent already durable

Approval required

Anything else stays blocked and shows the complete plan.

  • Objective and expected artifact for each step
  • Exact harness and model/config route
  • Capabilities and workspace/resource
  • Cost and runtime ceiling
  • Acceptance check
  • Rollback or safe-stop point
