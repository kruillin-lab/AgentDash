Approved design · execution plan

One CEO. One PM. The right worker for every action.

AgentDash becomes the control plane above Hermes and CodeG: you speak normally to a project manager, every executable request becomes a visible card, and the controller keeps the work moving until it is truly complete or needs a real business decision.

18 ordered tasks 15 specified route candidates 5 unresolved templates 1 disabled item Dispatch NO-GO until proof
The governing rule: one CEO approval activates one complete, finite mandate. Retries, scoped remediation, independent verification, and exact finalization continue inside that mandate without repeatedly interrupting you.
System ownership

Management is separated from authority and work.

The PM can explain and propose. Only the deterministic controller can authorize. Every model-backed action lives in CodeG and stays inspectable.

01

CEO

States goals in ordinary language, approves a complete high-stakes mandate, and answers typed business decisions.

Never asked to diagnose transport or retry mechanics.
02

Hermes PM

Clarifies, recalls reviewed context, drafts TaskSpec and route proposals, and summarizes original receipts.

No shell, files, browser, research, worker delegation, or lifecycle authority.
03

AgentDash controller

Validates finite paths, budgets, evidence, CEO provenance, one-time authority, outcomes, reconciliation, and finalization.

No model discretion inside lifecycle transitions.
04

CodeG workers

Run the exact harness/model route, expose original activity, and bind every action to its exact session and artifacts.

Never self-approve or finalize outside the command.

Board truth

Progress is a phase, not a blanket tag.

The canonical Hermes state stays visible as a chip; the CEO lane is a simple projection with strict membership rules.

Ready

Proposed, scheduled, or ready cards. Approval-required work has no worker session before activation.

Exact source state remains visible.

Working

Execute, retry, remediate, verify, finalize, cancel, or reconcile—with attempt, cost, elapsed time, and next scheduled action.

Only while a bounded next mechanism exists.

Needs you

Only a typed business/authority decision with the proposed TaskSpec revision and consequences.

Never technical ambiguity.

Stopped

Failed, timed out, budget exhausted, cancelled, route unavailable, or inconsistent. Receipts and unresolved identities remain visible.

Reconciliation pending stays here.

Completed

Successful action, verification, exact finalization, and aggregate receipts are all durable.

Success only.

Execution handoff

The first implementation move is proof, not launch.

Recommended execution style

Run the 18 tasks sequentially with a fresh bounded worker per task and a review checkpoint after each commit. External Hermes and CodeG changes live in their own clean worktrees; AgentDash remains the integration authority.

Stop immediately when

  • An exact path or interface differs
  • A command could duplicate work
  • A route is merely listed, not qualified
